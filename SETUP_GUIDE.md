# 🎈 The Simple Party Guide (For Parents & Friends)

> A zero-jargon, step-by-step checklist for setting up and running the NFC wristbands at the birthday party.

---

## 🕒 Part 1: Before the Party (Setting Up the 100 Stickers)

**What you need:**
- 1 Android phone with Chrome *(Android is needed just for the 5-minute setup because it has open NFC writing).*
- Your 100 stickers & bracelets.
- 1 Sharpie / pen.

### Steps:
1. Open the party website on the Android phone.
2. Tap the **Admin** tab at the bottom right.
3. Enter the PIN: **`1031`**.
4. Tap the button that says **"NFC Batch Writer"**.
5. Take your first sticker. Hold it flat against the back of your phone near the camera:
   - *BEEP!* The screen flashes green: **"Written successfully!"**
   - The screen automatically moves to sticker #2.
6. Write **`#1`** on the back of the sticker with your pen, then slide or stick it into Bracelet #1.
7. Repeat for stickers 2 to 100.
   - *Tip: It takes about 2 to 3 seconds per sticker. You'll be done in 5 minutes!*

> 💡 **The "Super Lazy" Shortcut:**
> If the door greeter and the barman are both using an Android phone with Chrome, **you don't even need to write anything to the stickers!** You can just stick the blank stickers straight into the bracelets. When you tap them at the door, the phone reads the factory serial number automatically!

---

## 🚪 Part 2: At The Door (Checking Guests In)

**Person in charge:** Door Greeter / Host

1. Tap the **Door** tab at the bottom of the screen.
2. When a guest arrives, grab the next bracelet (e.g. Bracelet #1).
3. Tap the bracelet to the back of the phone.
4. Type the guest's name (e.g. *Kasia*).
5. Pick their category:
   - **Standard (18+)**: Gets the normal drink limit (e.g. 5 drinks).
   - **Driver / Minor**: Hard-locked to **0 alcohol** (water and soft drinks only).
   - **VIP / Host**: Unlimited drinks.
6. Tap the big black button: **"Admit Guest & Link Wristband"**.
   - The phone chimes, confetti pops, and the wristband is now live!
7. Give the guest the bracelet and let them in.

---

## 🍸 Part 3: At The Bar (Serving Drinks)

**Person in charge:** The Barman

1. Open the phone to the **Bar** tab.
2. Stand the phone up on the bar counter (lean it against a cup or stand so the screen faces you).
3. When someone asks for a drink:
   - Ask them to touch their wristband to the back or top of your phone.
4. Look at the screen:
   - 🟢 **WHITE / GREEN (Approved)**:
     - You will see their name: *e.g. "Kasia - Drink 2 of 5 (3 left)"*.
     - A pleasant chime plays.
     - **Serve their drink!**
   - 🟡 **AMBER WARNING**:
     - *e.g. "Kasia - 5 of 5 (Final Drink of the Night)"*.
     - Remind them: *"This is your last drink tonight!"*
   - 🔴 **FLASHING RED (Limit Reached)**:
     - A loud alarm buzzer sounds.
     - Screen says: **"DO NOT SERVE ALCOHOL - LIMIT REACHED"**.
     - **Do NOT give them alcohol.** Offer them a free water or cola instead (tap *"Serve Soft Drink"*).

### Helpful Barman Tricks:
- **Accidental double-tap?** There is a 10-second yellow button that says **"Undo Accidental Tap"**. Tap it and the drink is cancelled.
- **Bracelet won't scan or got wet?** Tap **"Search Guest"**, type their name or bracelet number, and tap their name to log the drink.

---

## 👑 Part 4: Parents / Supervisor Controls

**Person in charge:** Parents / Birthday Girl's Family

1. Tap **Admin** at the bottom right.
2. Enter the PIN: **`1031`**.

### Things you can do anytime:
- **Change the drink limit**: Want people to have max 4 drinks instead of 5? Change the number and tap **"Apply Rules"**. It updates on the barman's phone instantly.
- **Grant someone an extra drink**: In the Admin table (or on the Barman screen), tap **"Supervisor Override"** to give +1 drink to a specific person.
- **Emergency Stop**: If things get out of hand, tap the red button **"Pause Bar Service"**. All drink taps across all phones will be immediately blocked until you unpause.
- **See who had what**: Scroll down to see a live list of everyone inside, how many drinks they’ve had, and who is cut off.
